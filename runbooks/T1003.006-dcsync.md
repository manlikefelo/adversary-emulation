# Runbook — T1003.006 DCSync

**Plan:** A · **Tactic:** Credential Access · **Target:** DC01 from WS01
**Detection expected:** Wazuh rule **100012** (Event 4662, replication GUID by a non-DC)

## What the technique does
Impersonates a Domain Controller and asks the real DC to replicate account secrets via
the directory-replication rights (`DS-Replication-Get-Changes[-All]`). Recovers NTLM
hashes for any/all accounts — including `krbtgt` (enabling Golden Tickets). In the lab,
`b.otieno` was granted replication rights on the domain root.

## Expected telemetry
- **DC01 Security 4662** — object-access with `Properties` containing
  `1131f6aa-…` / `1131f6ad-…` (Get-Changes / Get-Changes-All) by a **non-DC** principal.
- Requires DS-Access auditing + a replication SACL on the DC (see the audit-policy note
